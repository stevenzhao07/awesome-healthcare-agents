# 🧠 Awesome Healthcare Agents & Multi-Agents
> A curated list of **AI Agents, frameworks, datasets, and research papers** focused on **Healthcare and Medicine**.

---

## 🩺 Overview
AI agents are increasingly used in healthcare to assist clinicians, automate administrative tasks, analyze medical data, and improve patient outcomes.  
This repository brings together the **best open-source projects, research, and tools** in one place to help researchers, developers, and healthcare innovators explore the field.

## 🤖 Agent Frameworks
Frameworks and libraries for building autonomous or semi-autonomous agents used in healthcare.

- [LangChain](https://github.com/hwchase17/langchain) — Framework for developing context-aware LLM-driven agents.
- [n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n) — Visual AI workflow builder platform.
- [OpenAI AgentiKit](https://openai.com/index/introducing-agentkit/) - Tools for building, deploying, and optimizing AI agents.
- [CrewAI](https://github.com/joaomdmoura/crewAI) — Multi-agent orchestration for task collaboration.
- [AutoGen](https://github.com/microsoft/autogen) — Multi-agent conversation framework by Microsoft.
- [LlamaIndex](https://github.com/run-llama/llama_index) — Data framework for retrieval-augmented applications.

---

## 🩺 Clinical Assistants
AI agents designed to assist medical professionals or patients directly.

- [Med-PaLM 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09617) — Google’s large language model fine-tuned for medical reasoning.
- [ChatDoctor](https://github.com/Kent0n-Li/ChatDoctor) — GPT-based medical consultation model.
- [ClinicalGPT](https://github.com/ClinicalGPT/ClinicalGPT) — Fine-tuned clinical assistant on real EMR and note data.
- [HealthGPT](https://github.com/StanfordBioinformatics/healthgpt) — Personalized health tracker and assistant.

---

## 💊 Medication & Prescription Agents
Systems for drug interaction analysis, prescription validation, or personalized medicine.

- [DrugGPT](https://github.com/medAI/druggpt) — LLM-based drug recommendation and interaction detection.
- [MediSpan APIs](https://www.medispan.com/) — Clinical decision support data for prescriptions.
- [RxNorm](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/umls/rxnorm/) — Unified drug vocabulary from the U.S. National Library of Medicine.

---

## 🧬 GenAI for Medical Imaging
Agents and models applied to medical imaging, pathology, and diagnostics.

- [BioMind](https://www.biomind.ai/) — AI for radiology and neurological diagnostics.
- [MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI) — PyTorch-based medical imaging framework by NVIDIA.
- [MedSegDiff](https://github.com/yuankaihuo/MedSegDiff) — Diffusion-based medical segmentation model.

## ⚙️ Compliance & Safety Tools
Frameworks ensuring privacy, explainability, and compliance with healthcare regulations (HIPAA, GDPR, etc.)

- [TensorFlow Privacy](https://github.com/tensorflow/privacy) — Differential privacy for ML models.
- [PySyft](https://github.com/OpenMined/PySyft) — Federated learning and privacy-preserving computation.
- [Intel SGX SDK](https://github.com/intel/linux-sgx) — Confidential computing toolkit for sensitive data.
